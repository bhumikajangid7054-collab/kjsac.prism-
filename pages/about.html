About the Department

The Department of Political Science at K. J. Somaiya College of Arts & Commerce is dedicated to nurturing informed, responsible, and critically aware citizens through quality education, academic research, and practical engagement with political and social realities. The department encourages students to understand governance, constitutional values, public policy, international relations, political thought, and democratic institutions through an interdisciplinary approach. By combining classroom learning with seminars, research activities, field visits, publications, and student-led initiatives, the department prepares learners to become analytical thinkers and socially responsible leaders capable of contributing meaningfully to public life and policymaking.

About PRISM

PRISM (Policy and Research Insights, with Society Matters) is the official academic platform of the Department of Political Science. It provides students and faculty with a common space to publish research, share policy analysis, explore geopolitical developments, and engage in meaningful academic dialogue. Through Data Labs, the Y.U.V.A. newsletter, research projects, conferences, seminars, and public discussions, PRISM promotes a culture of inquiry, evidence-based thinking, and intellectual collaboration while connecting academic knowledge with contemporary societal challenges.

Vision

To develop socially conscious, research-oriented, and ethically responsible individuals equipped with the knowledge and analytical skills necessary to understand governance, public policy, and global political developments while contributing positively to society.

Mission

• Promote academic excellence through quality teaching and research.

• Encourage critical thinking and evidence-based policy analysis.

• Foster leadership, civic responsibility, and democratic values.

• Create opportunities for experiential learning through publications, seminars, and student initiatives.

• Build an inclusive academic environment that encourages innovation, collaboration, and lifelong learning.
