Events

The Department of Political Science organises academic, cultural, research and leadership-oriented events throughout the academic year. These initiatives encourage critical thinking, public engagement and experiential learning while strengthening the student community.

Aawaz-e-Youth

Awaaz - ae - Youth is a Flagship Event of the Association of Political Science at K J Somaiya College of Arts and Commerce. It is a two day fest curated for student engagement in actionable political science. The events include Act like a Politician, Debate, Open Mic, Political Quiz and several others. It represents the diverse initiatives of youth in the emerging political landscape.

Youth Parliament

Youth parliament is an inter-collegiate simulation of parliamentary proceedings that brings democracy to life. Students experience how debates, discussions and decision making unfolds inside the house.

Farewell 2026

Farewell was a notably nostalgic celebration the A. Y 2025-26 . It highlighted the hardwork, dedication and passionate efforts taken by the previous batch of the Department of Political Science. The graduating batch left behind their glorious legacy with the junior batches to carry forward.

PSA PRELUDE 2026-27

PSA PRELUDE 2026-27 is the commencement ceremony conducted by the Department of Political Science at K J Somaiya College of Arts and Commerce , to mark the beginning of the academic year. The students begin their fresh academic journey with a roadmap set for the coming year. In this ceremony, a student friendly website - PRISM along with a newsletter - YUVA has been launched by the Political Science Association in order to create a platform for interchange of relevant ideas and issues. The ceremony initiates and provides framework for this year's departmental activities.
