Events

The Department of Political Science organises academic, cultural, research and leadership-oriented events throughout the academic year. These initiatives encourage critical thinking, public engagement and experiential learning while strengthening the student community.

Aawaz-e-Youth

The flagship annual event of the Political Science Association celebrating dialogue, creativity and student participation.

Youth Parliament

A simulation of parliamentary proceedings that develops leadership, public speaking and policymaking skills.

Farewell 2026

A celebration dedicated to honouring the graduating students of the Department of Political Science.

Inauguration Ceremony 2026-27

The official inauguration of the Political Science Association and the commencement of a new academic session.
