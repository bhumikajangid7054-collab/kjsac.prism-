Y.U.V.A.

Youth Unified for Vision and Analysis (Y.U.V.A.) is the official newsletter published by the Political Science Association. To complement the vision behind P.R.I.S.M., the newsletter presents a synthesis of contemporary political developments, focusing on geopolitics, governance, public policy and literary recommendations that contribute in articulate capacity-building. Designed for students, teaching faculty and research scholars, it maintains a structured format of analytical and descriptive content. Published on a monthly basis, Y.U.V.A. aims to deliver onto the promise of P.R.I.S.M., bridging classroom learning with the contemporary world discourse.
