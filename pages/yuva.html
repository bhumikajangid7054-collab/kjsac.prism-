Y.U.V.A.

Y.U.V.A. (Youth Understanding Voices and Analysis) is the official newsletter of the Department of Political Science under P.R.I.S.M. It serves as a platform for students to publish articles, opinions, policy analyses, research summaries and contemporary political insights, encouraging academic writing, informed dialogue and intellectual engagement.
