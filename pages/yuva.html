Y.U.V.A.

Youth Unified for Vision and Analysis

Y.U.V.A. is the official newsletter of the Department of Political Science, providing students and faculty with a platform to publish research articles, opinion pieces, policy analyses, interviews, book reviews and academic discussions on contemporary political and social issues.

About the Newsletter

The Y.U.V.A. newsletter encourages students to engage in research, critical writing and informed public discourse. Every edition showcases original contributions from students, faculty members and invited experts while highlighting departmental activities and academic achievements.
