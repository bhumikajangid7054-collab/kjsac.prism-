About PRISM

PRISM (Policy and Research Insights, with Society Matters) is the official academic platform of the Department of Political Science, K. J. Somaiya College of Arts & Commerce. It promotes research, policy discussions, student publications, and analytical perspectives on governance, geopolitics, public policy, and society while encouraging academic excellence and informed civic engagement.
