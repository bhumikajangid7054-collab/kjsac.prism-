About PRISM

PRISM (Policy and Research Insights, with Society Matters) is the official academic platform of the Department of Political Science at K. J. Somaiya College of Arts & Commerce. It is designed to cultivate research, critical thinking, and informed discussions on governance, public policy, international relations, constitutional studies, and contemporary political developments. Through publications, research initiatives, events, and collaborative learning, PRISM encourages students to engage with real-world issues while strengthening analytical and academic skills.
